# The Obsidian vault is the source of truth

Thoughts, their Status and their Project live only as Markdown notes in an Obsidian vault, with no separate application database. Obsidian already provides relationships and graphing, and notes stay readable and editable without this app, so a change made in Obsidian must show up in the app and the other way round. The vault location is configurable because this may become a product for other people.

One exception: a hidden `.thoughts/` folder in the vault holds what doesn't belong in a note: AI cards, pending suggestions, the search and embedding indexes, and the cost log. Accepted Connections and dismissals stay as ordinary notes in `Connections/`, so the vault alone still records every decision the person made.
